Privacy Policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

LIIT Lighting (“LIIT,” “we,” “us”) is a family-owned permanent outdoor lighting company serving the Des Moines metro and the Iowa Great Lakes. This policy explains what information we collect through liitlighting.com and when you contact us, and how we use it.

Information you give us

When you send us your lighting plan through the form on our website, we receive the details you enter:

  • Your name, and your phone number and/or email address
  • Your city and area, if you add them
  • The choices you select, such as property type, lighting style, coverage, timing and preferred visit time

When you call, text or email us, we receive your phone number or email address and whatever you include in your message. If you use the “Text us your plan” button, your phone’s messaging app sends the message; nothing is sent until you tap Send.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you request and the time of your visit. These server logs are used to keep the site running and secure.

Our website does not currently use cookies, analytics tools or advertising trackers. If that changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required.

Our form temporarily keeps a scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address on our server to limit spam submissions. It is not used for any other purpose and expires within an hour.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and schedule your design visit
  • To prepare your quote and, if you hire us, to install and support your lighting
  • To keep records of our work and meet our legal and tax obligations

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Who else handles it

We rely on a few service providers to run our business, and they process information on our behalf:

  • Our web host (Namecheap), which hosts this website and delivers form submissions
  • Our email provider (Google Gmail), where form submissions and emails are received
  • Our phone carrier, for calls and text messages

We may also share information if required by law, or to protect our rights, our customers or others.

Text messages

If you text us, we will reply by text about your inquiry, visit or project. We do not send marketing text messages unless you have asked to receive them. You can tell us to stop texting you at any time.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry information for as long as needed to respond to you and follow up on your project. If you become a customer, we keep project records for as long as needed for warranty, service, legal and tax purposes.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what information we have about you, to correct it, or to delete it, subject to any records we are required to keep. Contact us using the details below.

Children

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
