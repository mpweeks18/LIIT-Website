Why voltage matters

It's the difference between a system that lasts and one that fades.

TruLight 48V

Every light holds six LEDs, with true RGBW color, so warm whites look clean and colors look rich. Each light is rated for 100,000 hours.

The 48V system carries power efficiently over long runs, helping keep brightness even from one end of your roofline to the other.

Motion sensors can be added for security lighting.

Lower-voltage systems Lower voltage loses more power over distance, so long runs can need extra power points to keep the far end as bright as the start.
Backed by a limited lifetime warranty. Lifetime means the rated life of each part: 100,000 hours for the lights (about 11.4 years) and five years for the control box. Applies to systems installed by a certified TruLight dealer — DIY kits aren’t covered.
