Questions

Commercial lighting, answered.

Can the lights match our brand colors?

Each light uses full RGBW color, so you can set colors that closely match your brand, then change them anytime from the app for seasons, promotions and events.

How is commercial pricing figured?

By the linear foot, based on your building and the areas you want lit. We walk the property with you and give you a fixed quote, with no obligation.

Can installation work around our business hours?

Yes. Tell us what works for your business, and we’ll plan the install timing with you.

What warranty comes with the system?

Systems installed by an authorized TruLight dealer are backed by a limited lifetime warranty. Lifetime means the rated life of each part: 100,000 hours for the lights (about 11.4 years) and five years for the control box. DIY kits aren’t covered.

Where do you install commercial lighting?

Across the Des Moines metro and the Iowa Great Lakes. Somewhere else in Iowa? Call or text us and we’ll talk it through.
