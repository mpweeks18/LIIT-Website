Questions

Des Moines homeowners ask.

How much does permanent lighting cost?

Every property is unique, so every quote is built for yours. We price by the linear foot, walk the property with you, and give you a fixed quote at your free visit. The number we sign is the number you pay.

How long does installation take?

Most installs take one to two days.

Can I use the lights for Hawkeyes or Cyclones game days?

Yes. Set black and gold, cardinal and gold, or any colors your family cheers for, and switch back to warm white when the game is over.

What warranty comes with the system?

Systems installed by an authorized TruLight dealer are backed by a limited lifetime warranty. Lifetime means the rated life of each part: 100,000 hours for the lights (about 11.4 years) and five years for the control box. DIY kits aren’t covered.

Do you light businesses too?

Yes. We install permanent lighting for storefronts, offices, restaurants and multi-family properties across the metro. See commercial lighting.
